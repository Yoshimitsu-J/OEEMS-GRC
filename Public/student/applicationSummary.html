  1. Valid Identification: Show the E-Document that OEEMS provides to ensure the person is the same person who applied.

  3. Assigned Seating: Examinees must sit only on their designated desk. Switching seats without authorization is grounds for disqualification.
  4. Electronic Gadgets: Smartphones, tablets, Bluetooth devices, and programmable calculators are prohibited inside of the exam facility.
  5. Silence: Absolute silence must be maintained. Any communication—verbal or non-verbal—between examinees will be treated as malpractice.
  6. Query Handling: If an examinee has a question regarding a PC malfunction or exam malfunction, they must raise their hand and wait for a facilitator.
  7. Cheating: Using any unauthorized materials or looking at another examinees PC.
  8. Impersonation: Having someone else take the exam on your behalf.
